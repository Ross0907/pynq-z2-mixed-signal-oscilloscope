PYNQ-Z2 OSCILLOSCOPE / MDO - AWG POWERDC LOAD MODEL
====================================================

The board-power model includes the finalized waveform-generator and local
bipolar-rail loads used for the completed Rev. A PowerDC analysis.

AWG / CLOCK LOADS
-----------------

U13 AD9102
  Rail: PYNQ_3V3
  Sink current: 0.030 A

Y2 125 MHz LVDS oscillator
  Rail: CLK3V3
  Sink current: 0.027 A

U12 ADA4817-2 AWG output amplifier
  Channel A active, channel B powered down
  Conservative device current: 0.022 A
  PYNQ_3V3 sink: 0.022 A
  -2V0 sink:     0.022 A

BIPOLAR RAIL MODEL
------------------

The LM27762-equivalent CP_3V3 input load includes the updated negative-rail
demand from the AWG output stage.

  SINK_U4_CP_3V3_GND = 0.277 A
  U4 -2V0 output load = 0.192 A
  U4 +3V0 output load = 0.084 A

The same split-supply amplifier current flows through the positive and
negative supply paths; the device current is not divided in half between
rails.
