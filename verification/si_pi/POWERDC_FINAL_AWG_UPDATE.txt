PYNQ-Z2 OSCILLOSCOPE / MDO — FINAL AWG POWERDC UPDATE
=====================================================
Frozen PCB baseline:
  Pynq_Oscilloscope(20260904-235630).zip
  Pynq_Oscilloscope-odb(9).zip

Previous accepted PowerDC baseline:
  Total PYNQ_3V3 draw        = 0.607028 A
  FL1 / CP_3V3               = 0.255 A
  FL3 / CLK3V3               = 0.027 A
  LM27762 +3V0 output        = 0.084 A
  LM27762 -2V0 output        = 0.170 A

Add the following AWG loads:

1) U13 AD9102
   Rail: PYNQ_3V3
   PowerDC sink: 0.030 A
   Rationale: conservative rounded value; ADI quotes 96.54 mW at 3.3 V,
   180 MSPS and 4 mA output (~29.3 mA total). Final design is only
   125 MSPS and ~2 mA full-scale, so 30 mA is conservative.

2) Y2 AK5DAF1 125 MHz LVDS oscillator
   Rail: CLK3V3
   PowerDC sink: 0.027 A

3) U12 ADA4817-2 AWG output amplifier
   Channel A active, channel B powered down.
   Use conservative combined device current = 0.022 A.

   PYNQ_3V3 sink: 0.022 A
   -2V0 sink:     0.022 A

   Do not halve this current between positive and negative rails: the same
   split-supply device current flows through both supply rails.

4) Update the LM27762 equivalent input load used by the existing manual
   PowerDC model because -2V0 now carries the extra U12 current.

   Existing:
     +3V0 load 84 mA + -2V0 load 170 mA + ~1 mA overhead = 255 mA

   New:
     +3V0 load 84 mA + -2V0 load 192 mA + ~1 mA overhead = 277 mA

   Therefore:
     SINK_U4_CP_3V3_GND = 0.277 A
     U4 -2V0 output load total = 0.192 A
     U4 +3V0 output load total = 0.084 A (unchanged)

Expected approximate new board totals before solving copper distribution:
  FL1 / CP_3V3        ~0.277 A
  FL3 / CLK3V3        ~0.054 A
  -2V0 total          ~0.192 A
  +3V0 total           0.084 A
  total PYNQ_3V3      ~0.708 A

The ~0.708 A figure is a planning estimate only. Final J3/J5 current sharing,
rail minima, trace/via current density and copper loss must come from the
new PowerDC solve on the final ODB geometry.

  PYNQ_3V3 minimum voltage
  CLK3V3 minimum voltage
  CP_3V3 minimum voltage
  -2V0 worst/most-positive voltage
  J3 current
  J5 current
  FL1 current
  FL3 current
  maximum trace current density
  maximum via current
  total PCB conductive loss
